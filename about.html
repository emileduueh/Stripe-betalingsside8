I started EmilKLMedia because I saw too many businesses wasting money on Meta ads that weren't built to convert. Broad targeting, generic creatives, no proper testing — and then blaming the platform when results didn't come.

My clients are typically e-commerce stores and service businesses who've tried running ads themselves (or worked with a generic agency) and are ready to see what a focused, data-driven approach can do. The average new client sees meaningful ROAS improvement within the first 60 days.

I keep my client list deliberately small. That means every account gets my full attention — not a junior account manager checking in once a week.
