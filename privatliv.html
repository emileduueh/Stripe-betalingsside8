What information we collect

We only collect information you provide to us directly when you:

How we use your information

We use your information solely to respond to your enquiry, prepare and conduct a potential meeting, and to follow up on a possible collaboration. We never sell or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-party services

We use the following services to operate the website and handle enquiries. When you use the contact form or the calendar, your information is also processed by these services:

  • Formspree (contact form) — privacy policy
  • Calendly (meeting booking) — privacy policy

Both services are based in the USA, so your information may be processed outside the EU/EEA. Both services are subject to their own security measures and data protection terms.

Retention

We only retain your information for as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry or carry out a collaboration, unless legislation requires longer retention (e.g. accounting rules in the event of a client relationship).

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to:

  • Access the information we hold about you
  • Have inaccurate information corrected
  • Have your information deleted
  • Object to our processing of your information
  • Lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority if you believe we are processing your information incorrectly

Contact us at emilklmedia@gmail.com if you wish to exercise any of your rights.

Cookies

Our website does not use its own tracking cookies. The Calendly calendar, which appears in a pop-up, may set its own technical cookies to remember your selected time slot — see Calendly's privacy policy for details.

Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
